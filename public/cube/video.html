CUBE VIDEO

< ERROR-CUBE

Turns a video from your phone or computer (MP4, MOV, WebM...) into a video the cube can play (MJPEG AVI with sound). It all happens in this browser: nothing is uploaded anywhere except to your cube. Keep this page on the screen while it works.

Easier: upload MP4s in error.blue/cube > FILES (they're converted on the way), or tap an MP4 on the cube and scan its QR code.

PICTURE
SHAPE
FRAMES / SECOND
QUALITY
SOUND
FROM / LENGTH

SEND goes through error.blue (works from anywhere, about 50 KB/s) into the SD card's VIDEO folder. Faster: SAVE it, then on the same WiFi open the cube's page http://<cube>/files (error.blue/cube > FILES > FAST) and drop it in VIDEO. On the cube: VIDEO.EXE.
