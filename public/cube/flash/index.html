ERROR-CUBE · INSTALL WITH USB

For the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16 (the white cube). Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. You only need this once: after that the cube gets new firmware from error.blue by itself, over WiFi.

Before the first install: BACK UP what's on the cube now (one file with the whole 16 MB flash), so you can always put it back exactly as it was.
  1. Plug the cube into this computer with a USB-C cable (a cable that carries data, not only power).
  2. Press INSTALL and pick the cube's port (USB JTAG/serial debug unit, or COMx).
  3. When asked, choose to erase the cube (the first time). The cube restarts with ERROR-DOS.
  4. No WiFi yet: on the cube open SYSTEM > WIFI, or join the WiFi ERROR-CUBE-XXXX with your phone and pick your WiFi there.
  5. Then open error.blue/cube on your phone and add the cube with its link code (ONLINE > LINK on the cube).

This browser can't talk to USB boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

It doesn't connect? Hold BOOT (the right button, "BOOT/-"), press and let go of PWR, let go of BOOT, then try again. Or unplug, hold BOOT, plug in, let go.

BACK TO THE ORIGINAL

Waveshare's own firmware (the app screen it came with), straight from Waveshare's GitHub. 16 MB, takes a few minutes.

Or put back your own backup: BACKUP & RESTORE.
