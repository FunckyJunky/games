HOW IT WORKS

This page is the light show: it plays it and sends your lamp the remote's keys while it runs, so keep it open (the screen stays on while it plays). Every change is one IR key and the board sends about 3 a second, so fast shows with deep fades can't be followed step by step: watch the IR bar, or press FIT. The lamp can't dim below about 30 %, so anything darker switches it OFF. Pressing OFF on the real remote stops the show.
