PRISM

Red, green and blue light. Tap a mirror to turn it. Light adds up where beams meet: red + green = yellow, green + blue = cyan, red + blue = pink, all three = white.

Light every crystal with exactly its colour. The crystal with the ring is your room: your lamp shows the light that reaches it, and goes off when nothing does.

Mirrors with a dot turn, dark ones don't. A half mirror lets light through and reflects it too.
