NIGHT THIEF

Steal the 5 jewels from the museum at night and get out by the green door. Guards with torches walk their rounds.

Light gives you away: in a lit room a guard sees you from far, in the dark only when his torch is on you. Stand at a switch and press E (or the round button) to turn a room's lights off. They come back on after 20 seconds.

Your lamp shows the light that falls on you: OFF = hidden. Yellow = a guard is getting suspicious. Red = caught. You can play by watching the room.

Arrows / WASD move · E or SPACE = switch · touch: the stick and E

LAMP: not linked
