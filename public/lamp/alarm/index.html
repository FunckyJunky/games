The lamp dims step by step in its colour now, then switches off.

HOW IT WORKS

The alarms live on the ESP32 with its own clock (from the internet, in your time zone). Nothing has to stay open: close this page, the phone can sleep. If the board has had no Wi-Fi since it was switched on, it has no clock and can't wake you. Press OFF on your real remote to stop a sunrise. The lamp can't dim below about 30 %, so a sunrise starts at the dimmest deep red.
