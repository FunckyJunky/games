⚇ NEARBY

See other MApP players who are within 200 m of you, walking or on their light bikes, with their names, and they see you.

Who counts as near is decided by your real GPS. While NEARBY is on, MApP sends your name, bike colour, where your avatar is (your GPS spot, or where you walk or teleport to in FREE) and your rough real position to MApP players within reach. In a far-away demo city you show as AWAY. Turn it off any time: then nothing is sent.

Tap the radar for the MAP: pick a player or a spot and teleport there, so you can walk around together. GPS brings you back to your real spot.
