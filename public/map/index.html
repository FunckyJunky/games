⚇ NEARBY

See other ZONE players who are within 200 m of you, walking or on their light bikes, with their names, and they see you.

While NEARBY is on and you play with GPS (GPS or AUTO), ZONE sends your live position, name and bike colour to ZONE players around you. In FREE roam or DEMO no position is sent: the others see you as AWAY at your last spot. Turn it off any time: then nothing is sent.
