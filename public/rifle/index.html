SPACERIFLE

Hold your phone flat like a rifle, top edge forward. Point it at another player and fire.

  1. Aim with the whole phone. Its top edge is the barrel. Turn until the red dot sits on a trooper.
  2. LOCKED means a shot hits. Dead centre is a critical hit (double damage).
  3. 3 hits derez a player. They come back after 10 seconds.
  4. Shield blocks shots for 4 s, but you can't fire while it's up.
  5. SPOT: can you see a player for real? Point the top edge at them and tap SPOT. Your compass fixes where the game puts them.
  6. GPS works best when players are 15 to 300 m apart, outdoors.

Arena

  1. Aim with the whole phone, held flat, top edge forward.
  2. SPOT a player you can really see: point straight at them and tap SPOT. Their trooper moves to where your compass says they are, and gets ◆. If you both spot each other, the two compass readings are averaged. It fades after 3 minutes, so spot again.
  3. Your steps and compass move you between GPS updates, so positions follow you smoothly.
  4. The drone is a practice target only you can see.
  5. The brackets around a trooper show where a shot counts. They are wider up close, because GPS is never exact.
  6. A red beam pointing at you and TARGETED means someone is aiming at you. Move, or raise your shield.
