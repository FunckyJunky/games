EAR-CUBE · INSTALL WITH USB

EAR-CUBE is the cube's third firmware: speech to text. For the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Already running ERROR-CUBE 1.5.4 or newer? No cable needed: on the cube open SYSTEM > UPDATE, press FW Other, then EAR-CUBE. It downloads EAR-CUBE over WiFi and restarts in it. Your WiFi, settings and files stay.
  1. Plug the cube into this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data.
  2. Press INSTALL and pick the cube's port (USB JTAG/serial debug unit, or COMx).
  3. Asked to erase? No keeps your WiFi, settings and files (C:\). Yes starts clean.
  4. The cube restarts in EAR-CUBE. No WiFi yet: join its own WiFi (EAR-CUBE-XXXX, the password is in SETUP > HOTSPOT) and open 192.168.4.1, WIFI tab.

This browser can't talk to USB boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

Or without a browser (Windows): EAR-CUBE-flash.zip: unzip it, plug the cube in, double-click flash.bat. It finds esptool (from the Arduino IDE, or pip) and the cube's port by itself, and erases nothing.
It doesn't connect? Hold BOOT (the right button, "BOOT/-"), press and let go of PWR, let go of BOOT, then try again.

BACK TO ERROR-CUBE

On the cube: SETUP > BACK TO ERROR-CUBE (over WiFi). Or with this cable: install ERROR-CUBE.
