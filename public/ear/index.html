C:\>EAR-CUBE

The third firmware for the ERROR-CUBE (Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16): the cube's two microphones listen to the talk around it, and error.blue writes it down. LIVE shows the words a few seconds after they're said; RECORD turns a long session (a lesson, a meeting, an interview) into a text file with the time of every line. The cube has its own WiFi, so a phone can read along and download the texts anywhere.

INSTALL WITH USB

From ERROR-CUBE (1.5.4+), no cable: SYSTEM > UPDATE > FW Other > EAR-CUBE. The cube downloads EAR-CUBE and restarts in it. Back: on EAR-CUBE, SETUP > BACK TO ERROR-CUBE. They share the WiFi, settings and files.

IN ERROR-CHAT (#EAR1)

Open ERROR-CHAT, channel EAR1 (or the channel set on the cube: SETUP > ERROR-CHAT). Every line the cube writes comes in as a chat message, and the cube says there when it starts, pauses or stops. EAR (top right, or type /ear) opens its panel:

THE CUBEOnline or not, recording / live / paused, the time, words, battery. Buttons: RECORD, LISTEN LIVE, PAUSE / GO ON, STOP, SCREEN OFF / ON, LOCK / UNLOCK BUTTONS (the touch screen and the buttons do nothing until you unlock). The cube looks every few seconds.
TRANSCRIPTSREAD (the one being recorded grows), DOWNLOAD, DELETE (from the channel and from the cube), SEND ALL AGAIN.

Everyone who knows the channel code can read the transcripts and use the buttons: on the cube you can pick a code of your own.

ON THE CUBE

LIVETap the microphone. What's said appears line by line, with the time. SAVE puts it in a file, CLEAR wipes the screen (swipe to confirm). P pauses. Needs the internet.
RECTap the red button. Every line goes into the ear folder on the TF card, in a file named after the date and time (ear\2026-11-27_00-10.txt; C:\ear without a card). P pauses. Works without internet too: the sound waits on the card and is written down when the internet is back, in order, even after a restart. KEEP THE SOUND also saves a WAV.
TEXTSThe transcripts, newest first. Tap = read (the one being recorded grows while you read), hold = delete.
SETUPLanguage (or AUTO), write it in English, sensitivity, microphone gain, left / right / both, pause length, MIC TEST; the hotspot (name, password, QR codes); WiFi; volume, light; updates; back to ERROR-CUBE.
BUTTONS- / + move or scroll, hold + = OK (start / stop), hold - = next tab, PWR = screen off / on. The screen can go off while it records.

ON YOUR PHONE

Join the cube's WiFi EAR-CUBE-XXXX (SETUP > HOTSPOT > PHONE: QR CODES shows a code for it, and its password), then open http://192.168.4.1. On the home WiFi: the cube's address (SETUP > WIFI). The page has:

LIVEStart LIVE or RECORD, stop, the text as it comes (big letters if you like), COPY ALL, SAVE AS A FILE.
TEXTSRead, download (.txt, and .wav when the sound was kept) and delete the transcripts.
SETTINGSThe same settings as on the cube.
WIFIConnect the cube to the internet (the home WiFi or a phone's hotspot), and install firmware.

HOW IT WORKS

The ES7210 sends both microphones at 44.1 kHz; the cube mixes them (or takes one), filters and resamples to 16 kHz, learns the background noise, and cuts the speech into pieces at its pauses (a long stretch at its quietest moment). Each piece goes as a WAV to /api/stt on error.blue, where Whisper large v3 turbo (Cloudflare Workers AI) writes it down. Nothing is kept on error.blue: the audio is only passed to Whisper; it counts the minutes a day (about 200, the free allowance). The transcripts stay on the cube. The hotspot gets a password at the first start: what's said near the cube is private.
