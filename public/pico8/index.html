PICO-CUBE

The second firmware for the ERROR-CUBE (Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16): it starts straight into a PICO-8 cart player. Tilt the cube to move, press its buttons to jump and shoot. The carts come from this page, over WiFi, one tap each.

INSTALL WITH USB carts.json

From ERROR-CUBE (1.5.3+), no cable: SYSTEM > UPDATE > P8 PICO-8. The cube downloads PICO-CUBE and restarts in it. Back: on PICO-CUBE, SETUP > BACK TO ERROR-CUBE. Both keep the same WiFi, settings and files.

ON THE CUBE

CARTSThe carts on the cube (C:\pico8) and on the TF card (A:\PICO8). Tap one to play; hold for DELETE. The one you played last is on top.
ONLINEThe carts below. Tap one: GET + PLAY. A green dot = it's on the cube, orange = a newer version is here.
SETUPTilt (how far for an arrow, turn left/right or up/down round), which button is O and X, CALIBRATE, a TILT TEST; volume, brightness; WiFi; updates; back to ERROR-CUBE.

PLAYING

TILTThe arrows. CALIBRATE once, holding the cube the way you like to play (else: the way you hold it when the cart starts).
− (BOOT)O  (Z on a keyboard)
+X
PWRPause: CONTINUE, RESTART, CALIBRATE, QUIT. A long touch on the screen does the same.

YOUR OWN CARTS

Any .p8 or .p8.png cart: put it in PICO8 on the TF card, or in C:\pico8 with the cube's own web page (open the cube's address, shown in SETUP > WIFI, on a phone or computer on the same WiFi, then FILES). Most carts run; very big or very busy ones can be slow or not start: the cube says why.

HOW IT WORKS

The emulator is FAKE-08 (MIT) on z8lua (MIT), trimmed for the ESP32-S3: the cart runs in its own task, 60 steps a second, sound at 22 kHz through the cube's speaker; the 128×128 picture is scaled to the 480×480 screen. The rest is ERROR-CUBE's own board code, drawing and interface, in PICO-8's colours. PICO-8 is a fantasy console by Lexaloffle Games; PICO-CUBE is a fan project and is not made by or connected to Lexaloffle. Get the real PICO-8 at lexaloffle.com to make your own carts.
